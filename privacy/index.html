Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

iNeedJimmy is a business name of Reverse Selling Limited Liability Company (Reverse Selling LLC) ("we," "us"). Jimmy Davis runs it. This policy explains what we collect on ineedjimmy.com, on our calls, and in our texts and emails, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • What you type into our forms and booking calendar: your name, email, mobile number, business name, business website, and your answers to our questions.
  • Basic information about how you use our site, like pages visited and the link you came from.
  • Your replies to our texts and emails, and notes from our calls.

How we use it

  • To book your call with Jimmy and send the confirmations and reminders for it.
  • To get ready for our call. We look at public information about your business, like your website and reviews, and use AI tools to help.
  • To follow up about working together, and to serve you if you become a client.
  • To answer your questions and improve our service.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information. We share it only with the service providers that help us run things, like our booking, email and texting platform, our payment processor (Stripe), our website host, and the AI tools we use. They may use it only to provide their service to us.

Text messages

If you check a texting box, we'll text the number you gave us. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with subcontractors that support our services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to stop texts.
  • Click "unsubscribe" in any email to stop emails.
  • Text us at (317) 827-7360 or use our contact page to see, correct or delete what we have about you.

Keeping it safe

We use reputable providers and take reasonable steps to protect your information. No system is perfect, so we can't promise perfect security.

Cookies and tracking

Our site and our booking calendar use cookies that are needed for them to work. We don't run third-party advertising trackers, and we don't sell this information. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but the booking calendar may not work without them.

Children

Our site, calls and text messages are for business owners 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top.
