My child is brand new. Where do we start?

Start by telling the team about your child. AirBound offers beginner tumbling and welcomes inquiries from new cheer athletes. Ask about the right starting point and current availability.

Can we visit before registering?

Yes. AirBound’s current website invites families to arrange a tour and get their questions answered. Contact the team to schedule a time.

How do we find the schedule and cost?

Use the current schedule and pricing link on AirBound’s official website. Ask about tuition, enrollment, clothing, and any team-specific costs before you choose.

Where is the gym?

2684 South 2000 West in Rexburg. Just off Archer Highway, next to Alpine Storage. Turn in at Coates Landscape Supply and continue straight back to AirBound.
