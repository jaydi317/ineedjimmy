The thinking underneath

Start with people.
Then design the page.

A child wants fun, progress, and a place in the group. A parent wants to understand the fit and feel comfortable taking the next step. The website has to help both.

01

Make the benefit feel real.

Show what movement can mean in a child’s life: courage, effort, and connection. Give the equipment a supporting role.

02

Make the first decision smaller.

“Find a starting point” is easier than choosing a level and registering before a parent has answers.

03

Give AirBound a recognizable voice.

Local competitors already offer tumbling, cheer, and active fun. These concepts explore three distinct reasons to feel drawn to AirBound.
