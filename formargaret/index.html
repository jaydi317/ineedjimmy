About

Most change programs fail quietly. Not because the strategy was wrong — because nobody in the room would name the thing everybody knew.

I'm now teaching that practice. The Field Method™ is the framework I've built from three decades of it: Read the Field. Hold the Room. Name the Undiscussable. Leave the Capacity.
