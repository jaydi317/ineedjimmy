Concept A · Editorial · People-First

The Bench

The product is the people. A quiet, editorial firm you would trust with the district's hardest week.

Frames Administrator Assistance as an on-call bench of proven Indiana school leaders. Serif typography, deep navy + cream + brass, calm and substantial. Feels like a private-client advisory firm your board president would recognize.

  • Headline centered on the human moment — a problem the district has never faced
  • Visible "bench" as the hero visual — six anonymized leaders with role labels
  • Services organized as three chapters, not a long menu
  • Case notes told as before → match → after, with public-record framing

Concept B · Civic Institution · Coverage-First

The Coverage Map

The product is the statewide network. A calm civic institution that has already been to most of these districts.

Frames Administrator Assistance as Indiana's district-response network. Bold sans-serif, deep charcoal + parchment + cardinal accent, with a stylized Indiana coverage map as the central visual. Feels institutional and unmistakably Indiana.

  • Headline centered on the "experienced answer" as the deliverable
  • Stylized Indiana map with 190+ district dots as the hero visual
  • Four-step response pathway (Intake → Match → Deploy → Hand-off) with timelines
  • "Capacity Index" replaces the traditional service menu
