The Idea, in One Sentence

When a school district faces a leadership or operating problem it has never solved before, Administrator Assistance finds someone who has already solved it — usually within days.

The Response Pathway

A single confidential call becomes an experienced answer inside your district.

Districts under pressure do not have time for slow-moving vendors. Our pathway is deliberately short so a superintendent or board can decide quickly, calmly, and privately.

When Districts Call

Six situations we have already lived through — on the other side of the desk.

Urgent

A leader has just resigned mid-year.

A vacant principal or central-office chair, an anxious staff, and a calendar that will not wait. We put an experienced interim in the building — usually within days.

The board is about to hire its next superintendent.

A search led by former superintendents themselves. A confidential pipeline, a defensible process, and a finalist the board can confirm publicly.

A referendum, audit, or budget cliff is approaching.

Former business managers and superintendents review the numbers, the assumptions, and the community message — before the board is asked to defend them.

Bargaining season is coming.

A chief negotiator with Indiana experience — at the table, at the study session, and in the community — so the district enters the room prepared.

The board wants a strategic plan the community will trust.

Facilitated retreats and planning by people who have run districts — not consultants speaking a language your board does not use.

Operations need an honest outside review.

Curriculum, transportation, technology, or central-office. A quiet audit by former practitioners, delivered to the superintendent first, then to the board.

Who We Are

Built by a superintendent, for superintendents and school boards.

Steven Wittenauer spent 37 years in Indiana public schools — as teacher, principal, assistant superintendent, and superintendent — before retiring from Benton Community School Corporation in December 2007. In January 2008 he co-founded Administrator Assistance with Dr. Gilbert Crimmins. In June 2023 he became sole owner and manager. The firm's culture reflects what he learned in the district office: get the right people in the room, tell the truth, and finish the work.

From the Founder

We built this company because the situations that most rattle a school district have almost always been solved before — just not by anyone currently on staff. Our job is to be the phone call a superintendent or board member can make on a hard morning, and to have the right experienced person on-site by the end of the week.

That is the promise we have kept to Indiana districts since 2008, and it is the promise we expect to keep for the districts calling us next month.

— Steven Wittenauer, Founder & Owner
