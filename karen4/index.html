Why only a few each week — honestly

No fake countdown. The truth: I learned I can only pour into a handful of women at a time and still come home with something left for my own life. So I keep just three days a week for this work. When the sessions are full, they're full.

Honest answers

Before you decide

What if I'm "too far gone" or too old for this?

There's no such thing. The women who move me most are the ones who've been overlooked the longest. If you read this far, she's still in there — that's exactly who this is for.

I don't have a big budget for clothes.

Good — because this isn't about spending a fortune. We start with what you already own and build from there. Any shopping list is short and intentional, not a shopping spree.

Do I have to lose weight first?

No. We dress and celebrate the body you have right now. Nearly every woman I work with is surprised how much changes without changing her size at all.

I live too far away.

Then we'll do your Becoming over Zoom or the phone. In-person is lovely, but the mirror works the same everywhere.
