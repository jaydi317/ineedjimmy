THE SHAPE

Three phases. Four doors. One root.

Every woman's struggle lives in three questions, in this order:

1 · “Why do I keep doing this?”
2 · “Why does nothing work?”
3 · “Who would I be without this?”

Phase 1 quiets the noise (food). Phase 2 rewrites the software (weight). Phase 3 reclaims her life (everything else). She doesn't descend into her problem — she ascends out of it. That's why it's drawn as a mountain.
