Why This Firm Exists

She watched a family go from flying high to cleaning swimming pools. She's protected doctors' money ever since.

Katherine was twelve when her stepfather's finances collapsed — from flying high to nearly bankrupt in a few weeks, taking the only job he could find: cleaning swimming pools at minimum wage. She's been working since she was ten. She never forgot what one bad financial stretch does to a family.

Decades later, as an attorney and CFP® advising the biggest names in financial services on ethics, she took a senior role at a firm serving doctors — and watched its president lie to physicians and push whatever paid the highest commission.

“I told him we may go bankrupt, but I no longer cared. I couldn't tolerate working for such unethical people another minute. I quit.”

In 2011 she launched MD Financial in Rhode Island — without knowing a single doctor in the state. Fifteen years and 650 physician families later, doctors from Hawaii to Cape Cod send their colleagues, their residents, and their own children to her firm.
