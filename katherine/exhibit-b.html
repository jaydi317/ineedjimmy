The Origin Story

The swimming pool that started a 650-doctor movement.

Katherine Vessenes was twelve when she learned what money can do to a family. Her stepfather went from flying high to nearly bankrupt in a matter of weeks — and took the only work he could find: cleaning swimming pools at minimum wage.

She became an attorney. Then a CFP®. Then the ethics expert the biggest names in financial services called when they needed to be kept honest. And then one day, inside a firm that "served" doctors, she watched the president lie to doctors and recommend whatever paid the fattest commission.

"I told him we may go bankrupt, but I no longer cared. I quit."

In 2011 she started over in Rhode Island, knowing zero doctors. Today 650 doctor families trust her firm — and 8 out of 10 new clients are sent by a doctor who already does. That's not marketing. That's a movement doctors built themselves, one referral at a time.
