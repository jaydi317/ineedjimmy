Why this is different from every other “customer research”

Every single line has a receipt.

Most agencies hand you a deck that says “customers struggle with emotional eating.” That's a summary. A summary is somebody's opinion about what people said.

The normal way

“Customers struggle with cravings.”

You can't write copy from that. You can't build a pre-call sequence from it. You can't even tell if it's true. Somebody read some things and formed an impression.

The part that proves it's real

There's also a file of what we refused to use.

Any system can produce more. A system you can trust is one that tells you what it threw away, and where it disagrees with itself.

DO-NOT-USE

Lines we will not put in copy

  • Raw trauma disclosures — women told us those things in confidence, not for a headline
  • Anything carrying a name, email, or phone number
  • Claims we can't trace back to a source
  • Health statements that would need a doctor to sign off
CONFLICTS

Where our own sources disagree

  • Written down in a file instead of smoothed over
  • Because a conflict is a map of where we don't actually know yet
  • And a research doc with no conflicts in it is one somebody sanded down
The restraint is the credential. Anyone can generate. Almost nobody deletes.
