Here's What Nobody Told You About Feeling Lost in Midlife

You're not broken. You're not ungrateful. And you're definitely not having a "crisis" — no matter what the magazines call it.

You're having an awakening. And I can prove it.

That feeling of "I don't know who I am anymore"? That's not the end of your story. That's the PREREQUISITE for the next chapter. You literally cannot become who you're meant to be while you're still clinging to who you were.

The identity you built — the career woman, the reliable one, the good mother, the person who always handled everything — she served you beautifully. She protected you. She got you here. And now? She's too small for you.

Your soul is trying to shed a skin that doesn't fit anymore. And instead of listening to it, you've been trying to squeeze back into it with self-help books, motivational podcasts, and a Target shopping cart you use like a therapist's couch.

But here's what I know after working with hundreds of women in exactly your position: You can't THINK your way out of this.

This isn't a thinking problem. It's a feeling problem. The blocks keeping you stuck aren't in your head — they're in your nervous system. They're in your body. They're in that knot in your stomach when someone asks what you do. They're in that impulse to spend money you don't need to spend. They're in the guilt you carry like a backpack full of rocks every time you dare to want something for yourself.

No amount of journaling is going to clear that. No amount of affirmations. No amount of "gratitude practice." And I say that as someone who loves all of those things — but I also know they're not enough. Not for this.

For this, you need a method that goes DEEPER than your brain. You need a method that works at the level where the blocks actually live.

That's why I created The Reclamation Method.

Let's Talk About the Thing Nobody Else Will Talk About: Your Money

I'm going to say something that might sting — but you need to hear it, and you won't hear it from anyone else in this space.

Your spending problem and your identity crisis are the same wound.

Every dollar you spend trying to fill the void — the online courses you never finish, the clothes you never wear, the "treat yourself" purchases that feel more like a cry for help than a celebration — that's not a budgeting problem. That's a POWER problem.

Money is power. It's always been power. And a woman who doesn't know who she is hemorrhages money the same way she hemorrhages energy, time, and attention — because she has no center to hold it all together.

When you reclaim your identity, you reclaim your financial power automatically. Because a woman who knows who she is doesn't need to buy her way to feeling worthy. She doesn't hand her credit card to a Target checkout clerk like she's handing over a permission slip to exist.

This is the conversation no other coach in this space is having. Everyone wants to talk about "finding your passion" and "discovering your purpose." Nobody wants to look you in the eye and say: "Show me your bank statement and I'll show you your identity crisis."

I will. Because I'm not here to make you feel good for an hour. I'm here to make you different forever.
