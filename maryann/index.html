The Practice

Healing that has been in your hands all along

Spring Forest Qigong is a gentle, modern form of an ancient Chinese practice — simple flowing movements, breath, and meditation that work with your body's own energy. No mats, no floor work, no strain. If you can sit in a chair, you can do this practice.

Steadier balance & ease of movement

Slow, deliberate movement that strengthens without strain — practiced standing or fully seated.

A calmer nervous system

Breath-centered practice that quiets worry and helps you sleep — most students feel it the very first class.

Energy that carries your whole day

Morning practice that opens energy blockages and leaves you clear instead of tired.

A spiritual practice that deepens with you

This is more than exercise. It is a path — and MaryAnn walks it with you.

Common Questions

Before you ask…

I've never done qigong. Will I keep up?

Yes. Spring Forest Qigong was designed so almost anyone can practice it — including people with physical challenges. Every movement can be done seated, and MaryAnn teaches to the room, not the clock.

What if I miss a class?

Every class is recorded into the Practice Library. Your membership means your practice travels with you.

How do I nominate someone for a sponsored seat?

Simply reply to any member email with their name and a way to reach them. MaryAnn handles the invitation personally and privately.

Is this religious?

No. Qigong is a health and energy practice with deep spiritual roots, welcoming to people of every faith — and none.
