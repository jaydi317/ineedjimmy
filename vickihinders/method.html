Vicki’s work pays attention to the feelings and responses around the eating, not just the food.

The goal is less blame and a more useful place to work. This is coaching, not a claim that one method explains every eating pattern.

The signature process

Four steps.
One place to begin.

01

See it.

Find a starting point. We look for what may be driving the eating. You don’t need to arrive with the right words; that is part of the work.

02

Set it down.

Work beneath the surface. Vicki brings hypnotherapy and energy work to the pattern you are exploring. No new food rules to memorize.

03

Seal it.

Support a different response. You practice responding to the familiar pull in a different way, with attention to how the pattern shows up elsewhere.

04

Stand.

Bring it into real life. Four o’clock. The drive home. The quiet house. You work on the hours the old pattern used to fill.

“Seal it” names a coaching step, not a guarantee that a pattern cannot return. Each woman’s experience will be different.

Meet Vicki

Quiet by nature.
Deeply attentive.

Vicki says she has noticed things about people for as long as she can remember — sometimes before they have the words for them.

For years, she kept that part of herself quiet. Her words for it: a “closet healer.”

She trained in hypnotherapy and energy work. Precise by nature, she wanted a way to work with what she perceived.

Now she has decided to bring that work into the open, with personal coaching for women who are tired of trying to handle this alone.

“I also pull God into it. It's not just me.”

Her faith is part of her approach. You are welcome to ask what that means in a session before deciding whether it is right for you.
