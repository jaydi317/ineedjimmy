# Three new directions for Vicki

## Start with More Living

It most directly answers Jimmy’s request: help Vicki see a credible, desirable coaching business taking shape. It combines a photographic personal-brand opening with editorial typography, a clear audience, repeated invitations, and a tangible coaching offer.

The other two are intentionally different: **The Set-Down Method** puts the named program and its process in the foreground; **Room for You** emphasizes intimacy, personal attention, and the six-woman group.

## What changed from the earlier pages

The earlier brief’s rigid visual rules are not the design authority for these three pages. Readable copy remains, but the narrow single-column template, sans-only rule, SVG placeholder count, and flat-color restriction no longer determine the design.

These concepts use original editorial photography, different type systems, full-width visual moments, asymmetrical sections, a designed offer, and a clearer personal brand. The old two mockups remain unchanged.

## The reference work

The supplied August 14 funnel list was read as research context. Its traffic estimates, business revenue, conversion judgments, and claimed measurements were not independently audited or treated as proven conversion results.

The following pages were opened on August 27, 2026:

| Reference | Observed design principle | How it informed Vicki’s pages |
|---|---|---|
| [Brittany Watkins](https://brittanywatkins.com/) | Large lifestyle photography, a recognizable personal brand, and an immediate audience-facing promise | More Living uses an aspirational photographic opening while keeping Vicki’s offer explicit |

| [Denise Duffield-Thomas’s Money Bootcamp](https://www.denisedt.com/bootcamp) | Distinctive program branding, founder presence, and specific descriptions of support | Designed program presentation and clear descriptions of private and group support |

These are design and structure references, not proof that these layouts will convert for Vicki. No endorsement by Russell Brunson, Dan Kennedy, Brittany Watkins, or any reference brand is implied. No other brand’s copy, testimonials, logos, or photography was reused.

## Honest mockup boundaries

- All three photographs are original AI-generated concepts. The woman is not Vicki or a client; the room is not an actual venue. The method image is a metaphor, not a medical explanation.
- Vicki’s own photo belongs in the marked brand-portrait area before a real launch.
- The proposed eight-week group, six-woman format, private first session, weekly calls, recordings, and between-call support come from the supplied brief.
- Price, dates, call length, support hours, recording privacy, credentials, and refund terms need Vicki’s confirmation before launching enrollment.
- Both of Vicki’s supplied quotes are preserved verbatim. Invitation copy is a draft for her review.
- There are no invented testimonials, press badges, before/after photos, client results, or fake scarcity. No guaranteed weight loss or permanent cure is promised.
- All application buttons open a mockup dialog. No personal information is collected, nothing is sent, and no payments are taken.

## How to open

Open `index.html` to compare all three, or open `editorial.html`, `method.html`, or `invitation.html` directly. Keep the `assets` folder beside them. Google Fonts loads the typefaces when online; the pages have local font fallbacks and no framework or installation requirement.
