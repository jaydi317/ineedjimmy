How long does it take to complete my return? ▼
A typical return (Federal + State) takes about 14 business days from drop-off to completion. Missing documents or returns requiring additional forms can extend this. Returns are processed first-in, first-out — earlier submissions get faster turnaround.
What documents do I need to provide? ▼
Generally: W-2s, all 1099 forms, Schedule K-1s, Form 1095 for health insurance, and records for HSA/IRA contributions, charitable donations, child care, and clean energy credits. Don't have everything yet? Send what you have — we'll let you know if anything's missing.
When should I turn in my documents? ▼
As soon as you have them! After the 3rd week of March, we can't guarantee filing by the deadline and will file an extension ($50 fee). Remember: extensions extend the filing deadline, not the payment deadline. Pay any amount owed by the original deadline to avoid penalties.
Do I need an appointment to drop off documents? ▼
Nope! Drop by anytime during business hours — no appointment needed. You can also upload through our secure Client Portal, mail them, or use the after-hours drop slot on our 2nd floor hallway door.
Can I email my tax documents? ▼
For security reasons, no — we don't accept documents via email. Please use our secure Client Portal to upload everything digitally, or drop off / mail physical copies.
How do I check my refund status? ▼
Use our Refund Tracker in the Client Quick Access section at the top of this page. It links directly to the IRS and state tracking tools so you can check anytime.
What happens if I miss the filing deadline? ▼
We'll file an extension on your behalf ($50 fee). But important: an extension only extends the time to file — NOT the time to pay. Submit any estimated payments by the original deadline to avoid late fees and penalties.
What if I can't come during business hours? ▼
We have a secure after-hours drop slot at our 2nd floor hallway door, clearly marked with signage. You can also upload documents 24/7 through the Client Portal.
