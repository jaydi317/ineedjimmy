The practice

Three ways to work
with what words miss.

Sara’s distinguishing idea is not that grief should disappear. It is that expression can give difficult emotions somewhere to go—and a guided practice can help people keep meeting life.

01

Art as a place to put the feeling.

No talent required. The value is in making the invisible visible enough to meet it differently.

02

Journaling as a place to hear yourself.

Prompts create room for honesty, memory, contradiction, and the questions that do not resolve quickly.

03

Movement as a way back into life.

Gentle embodied expression helps grief become something you can move with—not only think about.
