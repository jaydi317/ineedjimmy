The Truth Nobody Talks About

Does This Sound Like You?

You didn't burn out because you stopped caring. You burned out because you never stopped.

By 10am I'm already running on fumes. I haven't eaten, I've broken up two fights, and a parent just emailed me about homework I haven't graded.

I have a kid who can't stop moving and one who won't speak, and I'm supposed to differentiate instruction for both while hitting state standards.

I go home and my own kids get the worst version of me. I snap at them over nothing and cry about it later.

Everyone says 'take care of yourself' but WHEN? I have 27 kids, 3 IEPs, and a meeting with the principal about test scores.

I dread Sunday nights. Actual stomach-in-knots dread. That's not normal and I know it.

I used to be the fun teacher. Now I'm just the tired teacher.
