About Joanne

I Wore the Armour Too

I grew up in Belfast during the Troubles. The eldest of four. I learned to disappear — to make myself invisible so I'd be safe. The shyness was my first armour. The weight came next.

I spent decades searching for answers. I qualified as a counsellor. I trained in art therapy, Reiki, hypnosis, NLP, mindfulness. I tried every diet ever invented. Nothing worked — until I discovered the truth: the weight was never the problem. It was my body's way of keeping me safe.

The moment I stopped fighting my body and started understanding it — the moment I finally felt safe — the weight began to release on its own. Not through restriction. Through freedom.
