About Joanne

I Know What It's Like to Hide Behind the Weight

I grew up in Belfast during the Troubles — the eldest of four in a working-class family. I learned very early that it was safer to be invisible. So I hid. In my shyness. In my silence. And behind my weight.

I spent decades gaining weight to protect myself from a world that felt dangerous — and then decades hating myself for it. I tried every diet. I qualified as a counsellor. I studied art therapy, Reiki, hypnosis, NLP, mindfulness. I collected every tool I could find.

But nothing changed until I discovered the one truth that set me free: the weight was never the problem. It was the armour. And the moment I felt safe enough to take it off, everything shifted.
