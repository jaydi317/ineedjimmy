About Ann

I Know What It's Like to Look Fine and Feel Lost

For years, I was the woman who had it all together on the outside while quietly falling apart on the inside. I tried therapy. I tried self-help. I tried being more positive, more disciplined, more everything. None of it touched what was really going on beneath the surface.

My journey through Internal Family Systems, psychedelic exploration, compassionate communication, and deep crisis work didn't just help me understand myself — it brought me home to myself. I discovered that healing isn't about fixing what's broken. It's about meeting what's been hidden with compassion, and building a relationship with the parts of you that have been protecting you all along.

Today, I bring 25+ combined years of experience in the healing arts to guide women through the same transformation. My approach isn't surface-level, and it isn't for everyone. But if you're a woman who has tried everything and knows something deeper needs to shift — you're in the right place.
