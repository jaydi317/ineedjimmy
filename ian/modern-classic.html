Good Questions

Everything couples ask first.

Will you actually be our DJ, or someone from your company?
Ian, personally. Digital Sound Services is not an agency — the person you meet, plan with, and email at 11pm about a song change is the same person running your reception.
We have a "do not play" list. Will it be respected?
To the letter. No song on your no-list gets played — not by request, not "just for a minute." Your night, your rules.
Our families have very different music tastes. Can you handle that?
That's the job. Blended cultures, three generations, country-meets-hip-hop — Ian builds sets that move between worlds so smoothly your guests just call it "great music."
What about the ceremony — can you cover music and mics outside?
Yes. Battery-powered wireless systems mean your vows are heard clearly in a field, on a waterfront, or anywhere else you're saying them.
How far in advance should we book?
Popular Saturdays go 9–14 months out, and Ian only takes one event per day. If you have a date, it's worth checking now.
