The Philosophy

A great wedding DJ is invisible until the exact moment he shouldn't be.

You won't hear corny DJ jokes at an Ian Lade wedding. You won't get a stranger from a roster, and you won't get a Top-40 playlist somebody hits play on.

You get one experienced professional who plans your entire night with you — then runs it live: reading your crowd, blending your cultures and generations, honoring your do-not-play list to the letter.

The result is a night that feels effortless. Because for you, it is.

House Rules

Thirty years distilled into six promises.

You get Ian.

Not an associate, not whoever's free. The DJ you plan with is the DJ at your wedding — one event per day, every time.

Your no-list is law.

Songs you've banned stay banned. No exceptions, no "but the crowd requested it."

Names, rehearsed.

Every introduction practiced in advance — because mispronouncing your grandmother's name isn't a small mistake.

The mic, used sparingly.

A clear, well-spoken emcee when the moment calls for it. Never a showman making your wedding about him.

Every era, every culture.

Motown to Bollywood to this summer's song of the summer — mixed so three generations share one floor.

Sound that just works.

Professional systems with backups, plus battery-powered wireless audio for vows in a vineyard, a field, or on the water.
