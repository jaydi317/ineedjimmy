How it works

The share’s journey, in three annotations.

1

Before

You transfer listed shares you believe in.

2

During

You receive capital at a fixed rate, for a fixed term, and keep the upside — and the exposure. If the price falls past an agreed level, you have time to top up.

3

After

Repay, and identical shares come home.

Non-recourse: if the stock falls short, you can hand back the shares and walk away from the loan, subject to your agreement.
