The first loan

A Michigan apple farmer had lean yields and few places to borrow. All he had was a block of bank stock. A lender named Al Christy structured a loan against it.

“They got three million bucks … and I got a hug from his wife and 1 percent of the transaction.”

Al Christy · Indianapolis Business Journal, 2008

Al Christy, 2022

IIIHow it works

How a loan works, in four seasons.

  1. i.

    Plant

    You transfer listed shares you believe in.

  2. ii.

    Grow

    You receive capital at a fixed rate, for a fixed term. Use it as you choose. You keep the upside — and the exposure.

  3. iii.

    Tend

    If the price falls past an agreed level, you have time to top up.

  4. iv.

    Harvest

    Repay, and identical shares come home.

Non-recourse: if the stock falls short, you can hand back the shares and walk away from the loan, subject to your agreement.
