The EquitiesFirst Creed

Tap to read

We believe in people who believe in what they built.

We started with one apple farm that needed a yes.

We were paid with a hug and one percent.

We’d make that deal again.

We have stood with our clients through every cycle:

the dotcom bust, 2008, the pandemic.

We are still here.

We lend against conviction, not around it.

We start with respect.

We are here to help.

We are genuinely invested, because we grow when you do.

From Indianapolis to Dubai to Hong Kong,

for the next twenty-five years and beyond:

everything in life is possible.

What you built is still the beginning.
