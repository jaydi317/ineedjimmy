Can't hear you?

How to unmute your mic

Windows / PC
  1. Find the microphone icon in the bottom-left corner of the Zoom window.
  2. If it has a red line through it, you're muted. Click it to unmute.
  3. Shortcut: press Alt + A to mute or unmute.

If your mic still isn't working

  1. Click the small arrow (^) next to the microphone icon.
  2. Make sure the right microphone is picked.
  3. Go to Windows Settings → Privacy → Microphone and make sure Zoom is allowed.
  4. Unplug your headset and plug it back in.
Mac
  1. Find the microphone icon in the bottom-left corner of the Zoom window.
  2. If it has a red line through it, you're muted. Click it to unmute.
  3. Shortcut: press ⌘ + Shift + A to mute or unmute.

If your mic still isn't working

  1. Click the small arrow (^) next to the microphone icon and pick the right mic.
  2. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and make sure Zoom is allowed.
  3. If you just updated macOS, you may need to allow Zoom again.
  4. Quit Zoom and open it again.
iPhone / iPad
  1. Tap your screen to show the toolbar at the bottom.
  2. Tap the microphone icon (bottom left) to unmute.
  3. If you see "Join Audio" instead of a mic, tap it and pick "Call Over Internet."

If your mic still isn't working

  1. Go to Settings → Privacy → Microphone and make sure Zoom is turned on.
  2. Close any other app using the mic, like FaceTime or Voice Memos.
  3. Try headphones that have a built-in mic.
  4. Swipe Zoom closed and open it again.
Android
  1. Tap your screen to show the toolbar.
  2. Tap the microphone icon at the bottom to unmute.
  3. If you see "Join Audio," tap it and pick "Call Over Internet."

If your mic still isn't working

  1. Go to Settings → Apps → Zoom → Permissions and turn on Microphone.
  2. Close any other app that might be using the mic.
  3. Try headphones that have a built-in mic.
  4. Close Zoom and open it again.
