🎙️ How to Unmute Your Microphone

💻 Windows / PC ▼
  1. Look for the microphone icon in the bottom-left corner of the Zoom window
  2. If there's a red line through it, you're muted — click it to unmute
  3. Keyboard shortcut: press Alt + A to toggle mute/unmute
If your mic isn't working:
  1. Click the small arrow (^) next to the microphone icon
  2. Make sure the correct microphone is selected
  3. Go to Windows Settings → Privacy → Microphone and make sure Zoom has permission
  4. Try unplugging and replugging your headset
🍎 Mac ▼
  1. Look for the microphone icon in the bottom-left corner of the Zoom window
  2. If there's a red line through it, you're muted — click it to unmute
  3. Keyboard shortcut: press ⌘ + Shift + A to toggle mute/unmute
If your mic isn't working:
  1. Click the small arrow (^) next to the microphone icon and select the correct mic
  2. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and make sure Zoom is allowed
  3. If you recently updated macOS, you may need to re-grant permission
  4. Try restarting Zoom
📱 iPhone / iPad ▼
  1. Tap your screen to show the toolbar at the bottom
  2. Tap the microphone icon (bottom-left) to unmute
  3. If you see "Join Audio" instead of a mic icon, tap it and select "Call Over Internet"
If your mic isn't working:
  1. Go to iPhone Settings → Privacy → Microphone and make sure Zoom is toggled ON
  2. Make sure no other app is using the mic (close FaceTime, Voice Memos, etc.)
  3. Try plugging in headphones with a built-in mic
  4. Force-close the Zoom app and reopen it
🤖 Android ▼
  1. Tap your screen to show the toolbar
  2. Tap the microphone icon at the bottom to unmute
  3. If you see "Join Audio", tap it and select "Call Over Internet"
If your mic isn't working:
  1. Go to Android Settings → Apps → Zoom → Permissions and enable Microphone
  2. Close any other apps that might be using the mic
  3. Try using headphones with a built-in mic
  4. Restart the Zoom app
