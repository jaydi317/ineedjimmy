Straight answers

Questions people ask Jim.

How long does it stay fresh?

About 7–8 days on the counter in its bag. It freezes well for about 6 months. Slice it first and toast straight from the freezer.

Can people with celiac disease eat it?

No. Jim's Amazing Bread is made from whole wheat. It contains wheat and gluten and is not safe for people with celiac disease.

What does "whole-cell wheat" mean?

It's the whole wheat kernel, gently crushed on a patented machine in Fort Recovery, Ohio, instead of being milled into fine flour.

How does shipping work?

Every 6-pack and 12-pack ships free. We bake, bag and ship from our own bakery in Indianapolis. 2-packs and 3-packs pay a small shipping fee.

Can I skip or cancel my subscription?

Yes, any time, online or by calling us. We email you 3 days before every box so there are no surprises.
