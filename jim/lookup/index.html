Yes, Jim's bread goes bad in about a week. That's the point.

Big bakeries ship bread through warehouses and trucks and onto shelves, so it has to stay soft for a long time. Look at the red tags on their labels. Some of them even print the reason: "to retard spoilage." "To extend freshness."

Jim's label has nothing like that on it. So it keeps 7 to 8 days on your counter. Freeze what you won't eat that week and it keeps about 6 months. No preservatives on the label, so the freezer does that job.
