2Every hole we found

Nine leaks. Most are a one-week fix.

None of these are your fault. Every one of them is costing you money, trust or a store deal.

Amazon · Legal risk Fix first

Two listings say “Gluten Free”

Including your 4-pack with the Amazon's Choice badge. Your bread contains wheat. One complaint can pull the listing, or worse.

Pull the tag, add “Contains: Wheat.”
Amazon · Legal risk Fix first

Eight listings make medical claims

Words like diabetes and leaky gut. Food can't say that. It's the first thing a store, a lawyer or a buyer will flag.

Rewrite around taste, six ingredients and how it's made.
Website + Amazon

“21 days” on seven pages

Real life is 7–8 days on the counter. People expect three weeks, see mold on day 9, and leave a bad review.

“Fresh 7–8 days on the counter. Freezes for 6 months.”
Google

Your map pin is in the Pacific Ocean

Plus a second, duplicate listing. Anyone searching “bread near me” can't find the bakery.

Move the pin, merge the duplicate. Twenty minutes.
Money

The books are bank statements

No bookkeeping, and business money mixed in with personal. A buyer pays for profit they can see on paper. Right now nobody can see it.

Separate account + simple monthly books.
Stores

No insurance certificate on file

Grocers and distributors ask for a liability insurance certificate before they'll stock you. We haven't seen yours.

Confirm your policy and get the certificate ready.
Stores + Amazon

What bigger buyers will ask for

Barcodes (GS1 UPC), a Nutrition Facts and allergen label, case size, a one-page sell sheet, shelf-life proof, Indiana wholesale registration. Amazon wants claims-clean listings under your own brand name. Today yours shows “BFYSYO.”

One “store-ready kit” we build together.
Shipping

Free shipping on small, faraway boxes

A 2-pack to the West Coast loses about $2 on your site and about $4.75 on Amazon.

Free shipping at $60+. Amazon sells 6- and 12-packs only.
Price

Same prices since 2018

Your costs went up for eight years. Your prices didn't. Rivals online charge $14.67–16.50 a loaf for smaller loaves.

Retail +5–8%. Test $7 wholesale with one store.
