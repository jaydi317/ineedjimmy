3. What about the AT&T deal at Costco?

The Costco offer sounds like “new iPhones for everyone and the bill cut in half.” Here’s the honest version:

Most of the savings is the free phone, not a cheaper plan. AT&T doesn’t hand you the phone free. It gives you a small credit on your bill every month for 3 years. If we leave before then, the credits stop and we owe what’s left on the phone.

Verizon has the same kind of phone deals for people who stay. Right now Verizon gives current customers up to about $1,000 off a new iPhone when you trade in your old one. So we don’t have to switch companies to get new phones.

Simple way to compare: Only look at the monthly plan price. On that, Verizon “Welcome” is cheaper than AT&T.

4. Phone words, explained simply

LineEach phone number on our account. We have 4 lines (4 phones).
PlanThe package that decides what each phone can do and what it costs every month. The phones themselves stay the same when the plan changes.
Watch lineLets an Apple Watch make calls and texts even when your iPhone is at home. It shares your phone number. About $10 a month each.
HotspotUsing your phone as Wi-Fi so a laptop or iPad can get online — handy on trips.
PerkAn optional extra you can add to one phone for a monthly price, like Disney+ or extra hotspot. You can turn perks on or off anytime.
Auto PayVerizon takes the bill from your bank automatically each month. All the prices on this page assume Auto Pay, because without it everything costs more.
Trade-in & bill creditsYou give the phone company your old phone, and they knock a little off the bill each month for 3 years to pay for the new one. Leave early and you owe the rest.
Account owner / Account managerThe account owner (Dad) controls the account. An account manager is someone the owner trusts to help — they can see the bill and make changes.
