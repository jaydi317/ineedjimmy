Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to complete my return?
A typical tax return (Federal and State) takes about 14 business days from arrival to completion. Missing documents or returns requiring additional forms can extend this timeline.
What documents do I need to submit?
Generally: W-2s, 1099 forms, Schedule K-1s, Form 1095-A/B/C for health insurance, and records of contributions to HSA/IRA accounts, charitable donations, child care costs, or clean energy purchases. Send what you have — we'll let you know if anything is missing.
When should I turn in my tax documents?
As soon as you have them! If you're missing a form or two, send what you have with a note about what's still coming. After the third week of March, we can't guarantee filing by the deadline and will file an extension ($50 fee).
Do I need an appointment to drop off documents?
No! Drop off anytime during business hours — no appointment needed. You can also upload via our secure portal, or use the after-hours drop slot on our 2nd floor hallway door.
Can I email my tax documents?
For security, we do not accept tax documents via email. Please use our secure Client Portal to upload documents, or drop them off / mail them to our office.
Where can I check my refund status?
Use our Refund Tracker tool — accessible right from the Client Hub above. It links directly to the IRS and state tracking tools.
What happens if I miss the filing deadline?
We'll file an extension on your behalf ($50 fee). Important: an extension only extends the filing deadline — it does NOT extend the payment deadline. If you owe, submit payment by the original deadline to avoid penalties.
What if I can't come during business hours?
We have a secure after-hours drop slot on our 2nd floor hallway door, clearly marked with Shaw & Associates signage. You can also upload documents anytime through the secure Client Portal.

Our Story

Family-Run.
Community-Focused.

Larry Shaw founded the firm in 2002 with a goal that went beyond taxes — he wanted to build a firm anyone would feel comfortable walking into. Gone was the suit and tie. In came Buddy, his dog, who joined him at the office daily.

By 2005, Lauren — Larry's daughter — joined the team. Her dog Harley came along too. She earned her Enrolled Agent license in 2008 and her CPA in 2016, building her own practice under the Shaw & Associates name.

Today, many of Larry's original clients from 2002 are still with us. That says everything about what kind of firm this is.
