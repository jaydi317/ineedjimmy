Question 02 · The energy

Which idea keeps pulling you back right now—even when you are supposed to be doing something else?

Studio2a, real-estate development, a newsletter, congressional trades, prediction markets, or something Jimmy never heard. Why that one?

Messy thinking is welcome.
Question 03 · The foundation

What makes money for you today—and what about it must never get broken?

Tell us how Studio2a and real-estate work fit together, who pays you, and what your best work looks like.

This helps us protect the real business while exploring.
Question 04 · The origin story

Tell us about a moment when you thought, “Why is this still so hard? Somebody should fix this.”

Where were you? What were you trying to do? What made the current way feel ridiculous?

The best businesses often begin inside one vivid annoyance.
Question 05 · The person

Who is the exact person you most want to help—and what are they saying in their head right before they need you?

One real person is better than a market segment. What are they afraid of, tired of, or trying to prove?

Use their language, not industry language.
Question 06 · The 24/7 problem

What exactly needs to keep watching when you are not there?

For the trading or prediction tools: what sources should it monitor, what counts as an opportunity, and what should happen when it finds one?

Walk us through one example from signal to action.
Question 07 · The evidence

What have you already built, collected, or learned that we should not start over?

Show us prototypes, spreadsheets, prompts, code, data sources, newsletters, notes, client lists, or half-working experiments. What works? What breaks?

Half-built is valuable. It tells us where the truth is.
Question 08 · The advantage

What do you understand, notice, or care about that most people building this would miss?

This can come from architecture, visualization, development, markets, design, or simply how your brain works.

This is often where the unfair advantage lives.
Question 09 · The proud reveal

What could we put in front of you next week that would make you say, “Yes. That is what I have been trying to explain”?

A working monitor? A beautiful demo? A clearer offer? A research report? A sales page? Describe what you would be proud to show someone.

Do not make it realistic yet. Make it true.
Question 10 · The first domino

If the Council could return only one finished thing before you and Jimmy meet, what should it be?

Choose the smallest finished thing that would teach us the most or create immediate momentum.

One first domino. The other ideas are not lost.
