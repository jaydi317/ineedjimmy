Meet Monica

She Helps Girls Remember Who They Are

Monica Bourassa is a certified life coach and the creator of The Spark Circle™ — a small-group coaching experience where teen girls discover their superpower, learn to protect it with real boundaries, and build a sisterhood that changes everything.

Through her proprietary "Mind Up" approach, Monica teaches girls how their brain actually works — in simple, empowering terms — so they can understand why they feel the way they do and take back control. Combined with identity work, boundary training, and the power of a small circle, Monica's method creates transformation that lasts a lifetime.

Her mission: to make sure no girl goes through her teen years thinking she's not enough.
