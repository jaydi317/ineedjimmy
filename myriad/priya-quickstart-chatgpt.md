## WHAT THE RESEARCH FOUND (the AI Council's market study, Aug 2026)

**The winning mechanics across the best martial arts schools in the US & UK:**
1. **A low-priced PAID trial beats "free"** at the quality end: e.g. "£29 for 2 weeks + FREE uniform — and if you're not happy, full refund AND the child keeps the uniform." Paid trials filter tyre-kickers, and the uniform makes the child feel they already belong. Almost NOBODY in the UK runs this — every club in Barnet says "first class free," so it's table stakes, not a differentiator.
2. **Parents buy character transformation, not taekwondo.** Confidence, focus, bully-proofing, discipline. The trigger moments: a bullying incident, school behaviour concerns, back-to-school (late August/September is the #1 sign-up month — which is NOW), and New Year.
3. **The trial is a guided experience, not a drop-in.** Schools that script the first visit (greeting by name, uniform presentation, a photo, a chat with the parent at the end, a clear invitation to join) convert 50–60% of trials into members vs 20–30% unscripted. Priya at the door IS this system — she just needs the script.
4. **Speed and follow-up win.** Reply to every enquiry within minutes if possible; follow up 5+ times over 2 weeks. Most clubs contact a lead once and give up.
5. **Belt gradings are the retention AND revenue engine.** A grading every 2–4 months (£20–30 in the UK), made ceremonial — certificates, photos, applause — keeps children for years and creates natural moments for referrals ("bring a friend to watch grading day").
6. **Family stacking:** siblings + parents training together multiplies revenue per family. (GKR Karate built a whole empire on this.) Myriad is UNIQUELY placed: a family of black belts teaching families.
7. **Referrals should be 20–25% of new students:** double-sided rewards (a free month for the referring family AND the new one), bring-a-friend weeks, buddy passes handed out at the emotional peak of a belt ceremony.

**Local landscape (Barnet):** ~6 rival clubs (Total TKD New Barnet, UKTD Finchley, Master Wolf's, North London Schools of TKD, White Crane Karate, Barnet Combat). Almost none publish prices; all lead with "free trial" and generic discipline/confidence copy. **Myriad has no website and no Google presence — it currently loses every Google search.** But it also means the bar is low: one good page + Google Business profile puts Myriad ahead.

**Typical outer-London kids pricing:** £40–£70/month for 2 classes/week; £7–£12 pay-as-you-go; uniform £25–£40; gradings £20–£30; licence £25–£40/yr. Barnet Combat (nearest competitor with public prices): kids £45/month.
