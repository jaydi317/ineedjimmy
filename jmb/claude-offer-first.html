Fair questions

Before you start your box

“Will it still be fresh when it gets here?”

Yes. It's baked, sliced and bagged at the Kenyon Street bakery, usually ships within 24 hours, and stays fresh up to 21 days with no preservatives. “2 day delivery great” — Gary M.

“What's actually in it?”

Whole-Cell Wheat, water, honey, flax, yeast and salt. That's the whole list. The sourdough uses date syrup and extra-virgin olive oil. The wheat comes from a certified seed farmer who doesn't use glyphosate.

“Is it gluten-free?”

No. It contains wheat and gluten and is not safe for anyone with celiac disease or a wheat allergy. Many customers with gluten sensitivities tell us they enjoy it, but check with your own doctor first.

“What if I end up with too much bread?”

Freeze it. It freezes very well. Or skip a box. CONFIRM: skip/pause on the subscription app
