From the man whose name is on the truck

I didn’t start this company to be big. I started it to be trusted.

In 1993, I was a kid with a wrench and something to prove. I spent four years in a real apprenticeship — learning the trade the slow, hard, right way — passed the State of Indiana exam, and earned the title that still means the most to me: master plumber.

Then I spent years working for other outfits. And I watched good people — your neighbors — get treated like a ticket number. Double-booked. Talked down to. Handed a bill for a “crew” when the man who actually knew the trade never set foot in the house.

I figured the people of Hamilton County deserved a plumber who’s actually accountable to them. So I built one.

In July of 2007, I put my own name on a truck. ALL-PRO. If a job went well, that was on me. If it ever went sideways, that was on me too. I wanted it that way — because that’s the only way you earn the kind of trust that lasts 18 years and three remodels.

Twenty years and thousands of homes later, that promise hasn’t changed. We still walk you through exactly what’s wrong and what it’ll cost before we ever touch a thing. And we still treat your home — and your dog — like we’d want our own treated.
