01 — The Playing Field

Three Tiers of Competition

The Washington D.C. court reporting market breaks into three distinct competitive tiers. Each tier competes differently, wins differently, and is vulnerable differently. Understanding them is the key to finding your lane — and Cassandra, your lane is wide open.

The short version: The nationals compete on scale and technology. The D.C. independents compete on relationships and reputation. Realtime Elite has the credentials to outposition both — by combining world-class specialization, a coordinated family team, and the personal touch that law firms actually prefer. Nobody else in this market offers all three.

02 — Tier 1: The National Giants

The Corporate Agencies

These are the 800-pound gorillas — private-equity-backed or large-scale firms with national footprints, technology platforms, and armies of contract reporters. They dominate through volume and convenience, but their size is also their vulnerability.

Veritext Legal Solutions
Market Leader

The largest court reporting company in North America. 40+ state-of-the-art facilities, proprietary technology platform (MyVeritext), HIPAA/PII compliance, remote deposition platform (Veritext Virtual). Handles everything from routine depositions to complex multi-party IP and pharmaceutical litigation. Has dedicated teams for asbestos, medical malpractice, and patent cases. Recently acquired Capital Reporting Company in D.C.

Where They Win

  • Nationwide coverage — one vendor for all offices
  • Proprietary tech platform for scheduling, transcripts, exhibits
  • Brand recognition and inbound volume
  • Conference room facilities in major cities
  • Enterprise-level data security (HIPAA, PII, SSAE 16)

Where They're Vulnerable

  • Impersonal — you get whoever's available, not who's best
  • Reporters are contractors, not vetted team members
  • No relationship continuity across depositions
  • Large bureaucracy = slower response to unique needs
  • Premium pricing without premium personal service
Lexitas Legal
National

National court reporting and litigation support firm. Offers AI-powered "Deposition Insights" for transcript analysis and summarization. Strong remote deposition platform. Covers international assignments. Heavy technology investment.

Where They Win

  • AI-powered deposition transcript analysis tools
  • National and international coverage
  • Online scheduling and transcript access
  • Full-service: reporting, video, interpreting, process serving

Where They're Vulnerable

  • Same impersonality problem as Veritext
  • Reporters are interchangeable — no continuity guarantee
  • AI tools are a supplement, not a relationship
  • Complex cases still need a human who knows the matter
U.S. Legal Support
National

Network of 5,000+ professional court reporters. RemoteDepo Pro platform for remote proceedings. Covers all practice areas. "All-inclusive litigation support company" positioning.

Where They Win

  • Massive reporter network — can staff almost anything
  • Full litigation support suite beyond just reporting
  • Strong D.C. presence for government work

Where They're Vulnerable

  • 5,000 reporters = zero guarantee of quality or fit
  • "All-inclusive" = master of none
  • No way to request the same reporter consistently

The pattern with nationals: They win on logistics and lose on relationships. Every law firm partner who's ever been burned by a no-show, an inexperienced reporter on a complex patent case, or a transcript that needed heavy corrections knows the pain. That pain is Realtime Elite's opening.

03 — Tier 2: The D.C. Independents

The Local Competitors

These are the firms that compete on the same emotional turf Cassandra should own: personal service, local expertise, reputation, and reliability. Studying them reveals what "good" looks like in the independent space — and where Realtime Elite can leapfrog them.

Capital Reporting Company
D.C. Headquartered

Award-winning D.C.-based firm, now partnered with Veritext for technology and national reach. Founded by two trial lawyers and a court reporter — "we understand what you need." Won National Law Journal "Best Court Reporting" three years running. Woman-owned, WBENC certified. Services include reporting, videography, videoconferencing, transcription, interpreting, CART/captioning, exhibit solutions, case management.

What They Do Right

  • Origin story: "founded by people who understand your side"
  • Awards prominently featured as social proof
  • Woman-owned certification (diversity requirements)
  • Government past-performance page (GSA credibility)
  • Strong SEO — multiple city-specific landing pages

Gaps Cassandra Can Exploit

  • Now Veritext-affiliated — lost independent positioning
  • Blog hasn't been updated since 2018
  • No international credentials highlighted
  • Founders are lawyers, not reporters — different trust signal
Olender Reporting
D.C. Independent

Founded 1986 as a one-man operation by Thomas W. Olender. Grew to become one of D.C.'s most reputable court reporting firms. Olender personally videotaped the Clinton impeachment depositions (Lewinsky, Jordan, Blumenthal). Certified Legal Videographer. Provides court reporting, videography, and deposition services.

What They Do Right

  • Powerful origin story — one man to institution
  • Clinton impeachment credential = instant authority
  • Dual capability: reporter + videographer
  • Decades of D.C. relationship equity

Gaps Cassandra Can Exploit

  • Founder-dependent — unclear succession plan
  • Website has SSL certificate issues
  • No highlighted international experience
  • Cassandra's institutional credentials (EU, UN, Congress) are comparable or stronger
Evans Reporting Service
Baltimore / D.C.

Full-service stenographic reporting agency covering the Baltimore/Washington D.C. corridor and Mid-Atlantic region. Provides depositions, trials, hearings, arbitrations. Experienced realtime reporters. National and international capabilities. Reliable, traditional positioning.

What They Do Right

  • Clear geographic focus: Baltimore-D.C.-Mid-Atlantic
  • Realtime expertise prominently featured
  • Expedited delivery capability

Gaps Cassandra Can Exploit

  • No unique origin story or personal brand
  • No videography team — refers out
  • No visible international credentials
  • No family-team coordination advantage

The pattern with D.C. independents: The successful ones all have a strong identity — whether it's an origin story (Olender), an awards collection (Capital), or geographic focus (Evans). None of them combine international institutional credentials, in-house videography, in-house IT, and complex litigation specialization. That's the gap.

04 — Tier 3: The Model to Follow

Regal Court Reporting — The Growth Blueprint

Regal isn't a D.C. competitor — they're based in California. They're here because their trajectory is the clearest illustration of what a husband-and-wife court reporting team can build when they pair elite skills with intentional marketing.

Regal Court Reporting
Growth Model

Founded 2007 by Stephanie Leslie (3 years of reporting experience) and Isaiah Leslie (sales and marketing). Husband-wife team. Now one of the last 100% court reporter-owned, steno-only firms in California. Actively acquiring other independent agencies. Expanded to Florida in 2025. Stephanie served as president of the California Deposition Reporters Association.

Their Service Stack (What They Built Over Time)

  • ✦ Court Reporting (Depositions & Trial)
  • ✦ Legal Videography
  • ✦ Certified Interpreters
  • ✦ Conference Rooms
  • ✦ Transcript Summaries
  • ✦ Deposition Officers
  • ✦ Trial Technology
  • ✦ Remote Deposition Suites
  • ✦ Online Scheduling
  • ✦ Acquisitions Program
  • ✦ Student Reporter Program
  • ✦ Press / Industry PR

Their Marketing Playbook

  • Origin story front-and-center: "Partners in Life and Business" — the husband-wife narrative is their brand identity.
  • Industry leadership: Stephanie's CalDRA presidency gives her authority positioning — she's not just running a business, she's shaping the profession.
  • Press releases as content marketing: Regular EINPresswire releases for team hires, expansion, conference appearances — building SEO and credibility simultaneously.
  • Acquisitions as growth strategy: Buying retiring firms — maintaining their client relationships while expanding Regal's footprint.
  • Tagline that positions: "It's always been about people." Family values vs. corporate machinery.
  • Blog, podcast, social media: Multi-channel presence that most court reporting firms completely ignore.

The Regal vs. Realtime Elite Comparison

Stephanie and Isaiah Leslie started Regal with 3 years of court reporting experience and sales/marketing skills. They had no international credentials, no institutional clients, and no videography capability on Day 1. Everything they have now was built over 18 years.

Cassandra and Joseph Ellis have 30+ years of court reporting and videography experience. They've reported for Congress, the EU, and the UN. They've covered proceedings across 15+ countries. They have in-house IT capability. Their daughter is a bilingual legal videographer.

Realtime Elite is starting from a position that took Regal 18 years to reach — and in a higher-value market.

06 — Gap Analysis

What's Missing — And What It Would Take to Fix

Realtime Elite already has the hard-to-replicate assets: expertise, credentials, team, and specialization. What's missing are the easy-to-build marketing and operational elements that every successful competitor has. Here's the gap:

🌐

Professional Website

Current site uses a default WordPress theme with placeholder content and "Hello World!" as the only blog post. Every competitor has a polished, professional web presence. This is the #1 gap — it's the digital front door for every law firm evaluating a new court reporting partner.

📅

Online Scheduling

Regal, Capital, Veritext, and every major competitor offer online deposition scheduling. Realtime Elite has a "Schedule a Deposition" page but it appears non-functional. Law firms expect instant or near-instant booking capability.

⭐

Social Proof

No testimonials, no case studies, no client logos, no awards displayed. Every successful competitor leads with proof. Cassandra's work for the House of Representatives, EU, and UN should be the most prominent thing on the site — it's more impressive than any award.

🔍

SEO & Discoverability

No blog content, no location-specific pages, no practice-area pages. When a D.C. attorney searches "patent deposition court reporter Washington DC," Realtime Elite is invisible. Capital Reporting has 16+ city-specific SEO pages.

📱

LinkedIn Presence

Regal actively publishes on LinkedIn, issues press releases, and positions Stephanie as an industry thought leader. Cassandra's 30+ years of experience represent a goldmine of industry credibility that's not being leveraged on any professional platform.

💰

Clear Service & Pricing Pages

Competitors clearly lay out what they offer with distinct service pages for each capability. Realtime Elite's court reporting page has general language but no specifics about delivery times, realtime capabilities, practice area expertise, or pricing transparency.

The good news: Every gap on this list is a marketing problem, not a capability problem. Cassandra doesn't need to learn anything new, hire anyone, or change how she works. She needs a professional web presence, clear service descriptions, and a way for law firms to find and book her. These are solvable problems — and they're the exact problems that, once solved, unlock the full revenue potential of a 30+ year career.

The Competitive Verdict

The national giants win on logistics but lose on relationships. The D.C. independents win on relationships but none of them combine international institutional credentials, in-house videography, in-house IT, complex litigation specialization, and bilingual capability in a single family-owned team.

That combination doesn't exist in this market. Until Realtime Elite builds its presence — and then it does.

The assets are world-class. The market is desperate. The only missing piece is visibility.
