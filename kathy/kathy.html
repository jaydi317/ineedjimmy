Meet Kathy

I know what it feels like to make yourself small for so long that you forget who you were before. I know the silence after leaving — the one that's supposed to feel like freedom but feels more like floating.

I also know what it feels like when your body finally lets go. When the tension drops. When you feel a "yes" in your gut for the first time in years. When you look in the mirror and don't flinch.

That's what I help women find — not through more talking, more analyzing, more self-help books. But through the body. Through EFT tapping, mindfulness, and somatic therapy. Through a proven process that goes where the trauma actually lives and releases it.

I created The Reclaim Method because I believe every woman who found the courage to leave deserves more than survival. She deserves a life that's finally, unapologetically hers.
