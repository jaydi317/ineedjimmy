## A NOTE ON HUMAN DESIGN — READ THIS BEFORE MARKETING IT

Claude: be straight with Ashley about this, because it matters and nobody else will tell her.

**The Council searched hard and found no coach successfully using Human Design with a physician audience.** That is either an open lane or a market that has already tested it and said no. We cannot tell which from the outside, and she should not bet her positioning on it either way.

**What the successful woo-adjacent players in this market actually do** — Robyn Tiger with yoga therapy, Jill Wener with tapping, Gail Gazelle with Buddhist mindfulness — is identical and worth copying exactly:

- **Lead with physiology, not with the modality's name.** Tiger sells yoga nidra as *"bottom-up autonomic nervous system homeostasis."* Wener sells EFT as *"profound changes in the brain and nervous system"* at points *"corresponding to areas used in acupuncture."*
- **Use three specific words: "evidence-based," "trauma-informed," "secular."** "Secular" is the underrated one — it kills the *is this religious?* objection before it forms.
- **Attach CME.** Nothing legitimizes a modality to a physician faster than their own board granting credit for it.
- **Lead with outcomes, not mechanism.**

**Applied to Ashley's stack:**
- **Tapping** → describe as a somatic / nervous-system regulation protocol. Cite Jill Wener as precedent. Never say "energy."
- **Hypnosis** → say **"clinical hypnosis"** or **"guided imagery."** Both are PubMed-indexed in physician populations; there is a published study on medical hypnosis training reducing burnout in anesthesiologists. Do not say "hypnotherapy" cold.
- **Human Design** → **use it as a private personalization instrument inside the program, not as a public-facing named modality.** Andrea Austin fills that exact slot with DiSC® and an Oura ring — she has already proven physicians will accept a typology *if it arrives as an assessment instrument.* That's the door.
- On the free tool, call it the **Physician Energy Blueprint**, be plainly honest in the fine print that it is built on Human Design and is a typology and not a diagnostic — *use it the way you'd use Myers-Briggs: as a mirror, not a lab value* — and let the intellectual honesty do the credibility work. **With physicians, owning the limitation IS the credibility move.**
- **The Life Coach School** → completely normalized in this market. No translation needed.
