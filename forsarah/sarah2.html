Meet Sarah

At 42, I found myself divorced and completely disconnected from my body. I had spent decades taking care of everyone else — and somewhere along the way, I forgot to take care of myself.

Yoga saved my life. Not the fitness-focused classes at the gym, but trauma-informed, nervous-system-centered movement that taught me how to feel safe in my own skin again.

Now, with 15 years of experience and deep training in yoga, Reiki, and somatic practices, I guide other midlife women through the same transformation. You don't have to stay stuck. Your body is waiting to welcome you home.
