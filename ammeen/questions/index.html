What the research found

Your record

  • BA, Middlebury College. JD, IU McKinney, with the Faculty Award for Outstanding Law Student. Editor-in-Chief of the Indiana International & Comparative Law Review.
  • Law clerk to Chief Justice Randall T. Shepard, Indiana Supreme Court. Then Baker & Daniels and Lewis & Kappes. You founded the firm in 2007.
  • Registered civil and family mediator. Court-appointed receiver in State ex rel. Castetter v. Blaine, Hamilton Superior Court 5, since 2023.

Credit unions

  • Indiana has 121 credit unions with about 2.8 million members and about $50 billion in assets.
  • A January 2026 survey of small credit union CEOs: exams are exhausting, and their first ask is "help with complex regulations."
  • The most visible Indianapolis firm marketing to credit unions is Krieg DeVault. Smaller credit unions look thinly served.

Mediation

  • The Indiana Commercial Court keeps a directory of 47 neutrals, with rates. Indianapolis-area neutrals charge $350 to $575 an hour. Getting listed starts with an email to the court's staff.
  • Lawyers pay more for a mediator who can give a case value the client will believe. A former Supreme Court clerk is close to that credential.

Receivers

  • Under Indiana law the court appoints the receiver, but the bank usually proposes the name. Its biggest fear is cost.
  • Downtown office buildings are in distress, and lenders have been proposing out-of-state receivers. A trusted local receiver has an opening.

Collections and AI

  • Industry benchmark (Clio Legal Trends): 93% of billed work gets collected. Clio Payments includes payment plans, automatic top-ups of trust deposits, and a "Pay Later" option.
  • The ABA's guidance on AI (Formal Opinion 512) centers on confidentiality and client consent. A short firm AI policy lets us use these tools safely.

Ways to package your services

These are built from offers other firms publish. The prices are anchors to test, not final numbers. We'll set real prices together.

Credit union counsel

  1. Counsel on Call. Fast written answers and a regulatory calendar, for a monthly fee.
  2. Exam Desk. On-call help plus support through the exam, with policy updates from a set bank of hours.
  3. Outside General Counsel. Board meetings, contracts, policies and BSA oversight, priced against the cost of a full-time hire.

Anchor: firms that publish monthly outside-counsel tiers charge about $1,500 to $6,500 a month.

Mediation

  1. Half-day session. 4 hours, prep included.
  2. Full day, priced per side. Briefs due ahead of time, and a written deal memo the same day.
  3. Private judging day. A flat day rate with a cancellation fee.

Anchors: $350 to $575 an hour locally. Some neutrals elsewhere publish $3,750 for a half day and $7,500 for a full day.

Receiver work

  1. Receiver Readiness Kit. Free to lenders.
  2. Receiver's counsel.
  3. Serve as receiver. A court-approved hourly rate, often with part held back to the end, plus management fees on property.

Trustee and estate planning

  1. Estate plans at flat fees. Essentials, Custom, and Tax-planned.
  2. Plan maintenance membership. Annual review, updates, and a family meeting soon after a death. About $1,250 a year is common.
  3. Named trustee. An onboarding fee, a monthly minimum, then hourly or a percentage of assets.
