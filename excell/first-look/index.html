Quick answers

Do you take insurance?

We are in network with Cigna only. We accept HSA and FSA, and auto-accident claims with a valid claim number or attorney lien. We do not accept Medicare or Medicaid. For other plans, we give you an itemized receipt to submit yourself. Insurance details

Do I have to commit to a 6–12 month plan?

No. Your plan is built on your goals and changes as you progress. Not a preset plan.

Can I be treated the same day as my exam?

Yes, as long as the exam shows no reason not to. Dr. McCray treats you right after the exam.

Do I have to have my neck adjusted?

No. If your neck isn't the problem, it doesn't get adjusted. If it is, there are other techniques too.

Do you take x-rays?

Only when there's a clinical reason to.
