What patients will notice

All of these come from CT Engage, the texting system the office already uses for appointment reminders. Patients can reply STOP to any text to stop getting them.

Starts today

Thank-you + review request

A short thank-you after their visit, in Dr. McCray's own words, with a link to leave a Google review. Every patient gets the same message. Nobody gets it more than once every 60 days.

"Thank you for visiting our office. We look forward to seeing you again. If you would like to leave us a review, click the link below."

Starts today

"We missed you" text

Same afternoon as a missed appointment. Friendly, no guilt, with the office number to rebook.

"Hi [name], we missed you at Excell Sports Chiropractic today. To rebook, call 317-661-3478 or reply here. Reply STOP to opt out."

Starts today

Check-in when someone drifts away

If a patient hasn't been in for a while (about 30–60 days depending on the visit type), they get a friendly "time for your next visit?" text or email.

Already working

Appointment reminders

No change. These already go out and work well (over 800 this month).

If a patient asks about the texts

"Those are from our office. You can reply STOP anytime to turn them off." If they replied STOP by mistake, the desk can ask ChiroTouch support to turn their texts back on.

For Debbie: the Front Desk page

One bookmark on the front desk computer called Front Desk: ineedjimmy.com/excell/desk. Three tabs. A few minutes a day. It saves Dr. McCray from re-checking your work, and it shows him it's done.

Every morning · 1 min

Morning start-up

  1. Pick your name at the top.
  2. Handle overnight emails, texts and voicemails.
  3. Tick each box (add a note if something's waiting), then tap Submit.
At closing · 3 min

Closing check

  1. In ChiroTouch, open Analytics → Reports → End of Day (EOD) Summary for today.
  2. Type in: charges, patients seen, no-shows, cancellations, collected, and items still needing billing.
  3. In Square, open today's Sales summary and type the Square total. Add cash, checks and other payments.
  4. The page shows Matches or Off by $X right away. If it's off, double-check before you submit.
  5. Tick voicemails / emails / texts returned, then tap Submit.
This week only

Call tally

  1. Each time the phone rings, tap the reason: new patient, price, insurance, book, reschedule, directions, services, records, billing, or other.
  2. Tapped the wrong one? Undo last tap.

This shows which calls take the most of your day, so we can take the repeat ones off your plate. It's not about replacing you.

One rule: totals only. Never type a patient's name, phone or anything about their care into the Front Desk page. Notes like "Square deposit missing $20" are perfect.

How to change or pause anything

All in CT Engage: in ChiroTouch, click CT Engage in the left menu (or go to chirotouch.patientengagepro.com). Changes save when you click Save.
