Step 2 · All day

As it happens

A patient doesn't show up: mark it "No Show" in ChiroTouch

Use No Show, not Cancel and not Delete. That one click makes the patient get a friendly "we missed you" text and email the next day, with a link to rebook online. If they called to cancel, mark it Cancel (no message goes out).

Every phone call (this week only): tap the reason

On the Call tally tab, tap the reason the caller gave. One tap = one call. For Other, type a few words (no names). Tapped the wrong one? Undo last tap.

This is just for one week, so Dr. McCray can see what people call about most.

Keep phone numbers and emails right in ChiroTouch

Appointment reminders, recall check-ins and review requests all go out automatically, using the cell number and email in ChiroTouch. A wrong number means a missed patient.

If something looks wrong

Quick fixes

It says "Not sent yet — will retry"

That's fine (usually the internet blinked). Don't type it again. The page keeps trying every 30 seconds while it's open, and again the next time it's opened.

A box turned red, or it says "Still needed"

Numbers only in number boxes (no letters). If something was zero, type 0.

I sent the wrong numbers

Submit the closing check again with the right numbers and type "correction" in the note. Dr. McCray will see both, and the later one counts.

The office is closed today

Skip it. Dr. McCray may get a "closing not done" reminder that evening. He can ignore it.

The page shows something strange

Take a screenshot with no patient names on screen and text it to Jimmy: 626-354-3229.
