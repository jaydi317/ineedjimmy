Questions you might have

Why did all of this stop in the first place?

The review, recall and missed-visit messages each had their "send by text" box unticked, so nothing could go out. I can't tell exactly when or why, but it's been that way since about August. Appointment reminders were never affected; those kept working the whole time.

Will patients get too many texts?

We've kept it light. A patient is asked for a review at most about once every 6 months, plus a reminder. Recall is for people who haven't booked their next visit, and it checks for an upcoming appointment before it texts. Every text has a STOP option.

Can an unhappy patient still post on Google?

Yes, always. Everyone sees the Google button. The "How can we improve?" button is just an extra way to reach you privately. Hiding Google from unhappy patients is called review gating, and Google's rules and the FTC's guidance are against it, so we don't do it.

Why did you change the Google replies?

Replying to a review in a way that confirms the person is a patient can create HIPAA risk, and a reply like "see you at your next visit" does that. The new replies just say thank you. (Not legal advice. Your compliance person is welcome to look over the wording.)

Does anyone have to reply to Google reviews now?

No. Replies post on their own, so nobody has to type them. Unhappy reviewers are invited to call the office, so those come in like any other phone call.

What happens if a patient texts back?

Replies come into CT Engage's message inbox, where the front desk already sees patient texts.

Does the front desk need to do anything new?

No new steps. They may see a few more texts back from patients, and the occasional call from someone who wants to talk.

Can we change the wording later?

Yes. Send me the wording you'd like and I'll update it in CT Engage.

Did you change anything in ChiroTouch itself?

No. Charts, billing and scheduling are untouched. Every change this week was inside CT Engage's messaging settings.

Is any patient information going into AI?

No. This week's changes were only made in CT Engage's settings, and no patient information was given to AI. For the nightly report, the plan is to send you totals only: charges, collections, visit counts. No names. I'll confirm with ChiroTouch before building it.

Does any of this cost more?

No. It's all part of CT Engage, which you already pay for. We just made it actually work.
