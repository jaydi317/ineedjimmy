Before you book

Questions

Do you take my insurance?

We’re in-network with Cigna, and we bill auto insurance with an open claim number or attorney lien. HSA and FSA cards are welcome. With other plans, you pay at the visit and we give you an itemized receipt to submit yourself. We don’t accept Medicare or Medicaid.

Will I have to sign up for a 6 to 12 month plan?

No. Your plan is based on your goals and changes as you improve.

Do you treat on the first visit?

Yes. If the exam shows no reason to wait, Dr. McCray treats you the same day.

Do I have to have my neck adjusted?

No. If your neck isn’t the problem, it isn’t adjusted. If it is, there are gentle options.

Do you take x-rays?

Only when they’re clinically needed.

I’m not an athlete. Is this for me?

Yes. Dr. McCray treats the Colts and the everyday active person: runners, parents, people who stand all day.
