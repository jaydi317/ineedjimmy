Before you book

Ask the questions
on your mind.

Do I need to be an athlete?+

No. Excell works with professional athletes and everyday active people. Tell the office about your concern and the activity you want to return to.

Will I have to commit to a long treatment plan?+

Excell’s FAQ says plans are based on individual goals and can change with progress, rather than a preset six-to-twelve-month course.

Can treatment start on the first visit?+

It can, when the examination finds no contraindication. Dr. McCray determines what is appropriate after examining you.

Does my neck have to be adjusted?+

Not if it is not your main concern. If it is, Excell’s FAQ says other techniques may be considered. Talk through your preferences with Dr. McCray.

What should I know about insurance?+

Excell is in-network with Cigna and accepts HSA/FSA payments. Other plans are not billed on your behalf. Call about your benefits and expected cost before your visit.
