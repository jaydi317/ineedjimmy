Not just an adjustment

We find the cause first.

Here, the exam decides what happens next.

  1. We listen and examine

    Orthopedic and neurological tests specific to what's bothering you, done before any treatment is recommended.

  2. We tell you what we found

    You hear what's going on and what Dr. McCray recommends. X-rays only when there's a clinical reason.

  3. We treat, or refer you out

    Care often starts the same visit. If another provider is the better fit, Dr. McCray tells you and refers you.

  • No 6 to 12 month contract
  • No neck adjustment you don't need
  • No X-rays without a reason
