Opening

Hi, this is Dr. Marcus McCray at Excell Sports Chiropractic, account ACT0000054633. I sent in a support ticket on October 26 about automating our End of Day report and haven't heard back, so I'm calling instead. I have a few quick questions about reports.

Question 1

Can it run on its own?

Is there any way to schedule the End of Day Summary to run automatically every day and be emailed to me, or saved as an Excel file?

If yes: ask them to walk you through setting it up right now, sent to docmmccray@gmail.com. That's the best possible answer, and we're done.
If no: ask "Is that coming, or is there any workaround, like a saved or subscribed report?" Then go to question 2.

Question 2

Any official way to get the numbers out?

Is there an official way to send our daily totals to another system? An API, a data feed, or a Marketplace partner that does reporting or analytics? Which ones, and what do they cost?

Write down any partner names and prices. If they offer a "full data export," that's a one-time copy that can take weeks. We don't need it, so you can pass.

Question 3 · the important one

Is our reports-only login OK to automate?

We created a staff login called "Excell Reports" that can only see Analytics. No charting, billing or scheduling. Is it OK under our agreement for a program on our own office computer to log in with that user once a day, run the End of Day Summary, and download it?

If yes: ask them to note it on your account or ticket, or email it to you. Getting that in writing is ideal.
If no: ask "What would you recommend instead?"
If they're not sure: ask "Can you check with someone who knows, or escalate it, and email me the answer?"

Does the End of Day Summary show how many new patients we saw that day? If not, which report does?
