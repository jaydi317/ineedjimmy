Before you apply

Good questions deserve calm answers.

Do I need to already know what’s wrong?

No. You can begin with what you notice — the times, feelings, or situations around eating. Finding the pattern is part of the work, not homework you must finish first.

What if therapy or dietitians didn’t work before?

Bring that history with you. We’ll look at what helped, what didn’t, and whether this approach fills a gap rather than asking you to repeat the same experience.

How is this different from another diet?

This is not another set of food rules. We work on reading your body, understanding the roots of the pattern, rebuilding trust, and creating support that lasts.
