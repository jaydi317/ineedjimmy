Getting this live. Cheap domain, free hosting.

Total cost for year one: about $12. That is the domain. The hosting is free forever.

Buy the domain

Go to Cloudflare Registrar or Namecheap. Search thebutterflymethod.com.

If it is taken, try carinadevilliers.com or thebutterflymethod.co.nz. Expect $10–$15 for the year.

Cloudflare sells at cost with no renewal jump. That is the one I'd pick.

Make a free GitHub account

Go to github.com and sign up. Username something like carinadevilliers.

It is free. You do not need to understand any code.

Create the repository

Click the + in the top right, then New repository.

Name it exactly: yourusername.github.io. Set it to Public. Click create.

Upload the page

Rename your chosen mockup file to index.html.

In the repository click Add file → Upload files, drag it in, then Commit changes.

Everything is inside that one file — no images to upload. It just works.

Switch the site on

Go to Settings → Pages. Under Source pick Deploy from a branch, branch main, folder / (root). Save.

Wait two minutes. Your site is now live at yourusername.github.io.

Point your domain at it

In Settings → Pages → Custom domain, type your domain and save. GitHub creates a CNAME file for you.

Then in your domain account add these A records for @:

185.199.108.153   185.199.109.153   185.199.110.153   185.199.111.153

And one CNAME for www pointing to yourusername.github.io.

Back in GitHub, tick Enforce HTTPS. Done. It is live and secure.

Add the booking link

Sign up free at TidyCal ($29 once, lifetime) or Calendly (free tier).

Create a 20 minute call called "A quiet 20 minutes with Carina."

Then find every href="#book" or href="#offer" in the file and swap it for your booking link.

Take payment

Open a free Stripe account. Create three Payment Links.

$297 one-off. $3,000 one-off. $1,100 × 3. $97 monthly subscription.

You do not need a shopping cart. You send the link on the call.

The first 90 days.

Days 1–14 — get findable

Site live. Booking link working. Stripe links made.

Then have ten conversations with women who fit. Not to sell. To listen and collect their exact words.

Days 15–45 — your first three

Never take a client under $1,000. That rule is not about the money.

It is about what happens in you when a woman pays you properly and then tells you it changed her life.

Record every session you are allowed to record. Ask one question at the end: what can you do now that you could not do before?

Days 46–90 — proof, then scale

Three real stories on the page, in their words, spelling mistakes and all. Then open The Flight Room.

Now the page does the qualifying and you just have the conversations. That is a business.
