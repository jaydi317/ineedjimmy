Featured Equipment

Optos Daytona Ultra-Widefield Imaging

Traditional retinal cameras capture about 45° of the back of your eye — like looking through a keyhole. Our Optos Daytona captures over 185° in a single, comfortable image.

This means we can detect conditions like diabetic retinopathy, retinal tears, macular degeneration, and glaucoma earlier — often before you notice any symptoms at all.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a Costco membership to get an eye exam?
Absolutely not. Our practice is independent — we just happen to be inside Costco. Anyone can walk in. You only need a membership to purchase glasses from Costco's optical department.
What insurance plans do you accept?
We accept VSP, Spectera/United Healthcare, and Costco employee plans. If you're not sure about yours, give us a call at (463) 238-2028 and we'll check for you.
Can I just walk in without an appointment?
Walk-ins are welcome when our schedule allows. For guaranteed availability, we recommend booking online or calling ahead. If you need to cancel, we ask for 24 hours notice.
What should I bring to my appointment?
Your insurance card and photo ID, any current glasses or contacts, and a list of medications you take. For VSP patients: the primary insurer's name, DOB, and last 4 of their SSN.
What is the ultra-widefield retinal imaging?
It's our Optos Daytona camera that captures 185° of the back of your eye — about 4x more than traditional cameras. It helps us catch diseases like diabetic retinopathy and glaucoma much earlier. It's quick, comfortable, and often reduces the need for dilation drops.
How often should I get my eyes checked?
Most adults should have a comprehensive exam every 1-2 years. Annual exams are recommended for contact lens wearers, diabetics, and anyone with a family history of eye disease. Kids should start at 6 months, then age 3, then before first grade.
Can I take my prescription to another optical shop?
Of course. At the end of every exam, we provide you with your full prescription. You're free to fill it anywhere you'd like. That said, Costco Optical offers excellent quality at very competitive prices.
Do you see children?
Yes! We provide comprehensive pediatric eye exams. Early detection of vision problems is crucial for children's development and learning.
