About Stacey

SM

Teacher. Counselor. Healer. Woman of Faith.

I started my career as a teacher and school counselor, working with at-risk kids who were acting out, shutting down, or just barely holding it together. What I kept seeing underneath the behavior was always the same thing: unprocessed trauma.

So I went deeper. I became a Licensed Professional Counselor. I trained in EMDR and Emotional Transformation Therapy. I spent 16+ years walking alongside people — from four-year-olds to adults — helping them heal what talk therapy alone couldn't touch.

But somewhere along the way, I realized that the women I was seeing in my office weren't so different from the kids I'd worked with years ago. They were still carrying the same wounds — just better at hiding them. The Still & Strong Method™ was born from that realization.
