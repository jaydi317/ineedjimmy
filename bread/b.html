Ingredient 1 of 6

Whole-Cell Wheat.

Most whole-wheat bread starts as heavily milled flour, which breaks the grain's cell walls. Ours is gently crushed, so more of the wheat cell stays intact. It gives every slice a hearty texture and 5 grams of fiber.

No glyphosate. Bought from a certified seed farmer.

Is it gluten-free?

No. It contains wheat and gluten and is not safe for anyone with celiac disease or a wheat allergy. Many customers with gluten sensitivities tell us they enjoy it, but check with your own doctor first.

How is Whole-Cell Wheat different from whole-wheat flour?

Most whole-wheat flour is heavily milled, which breaks the grain's cell walls. Whole-Cell Wheat is gently crushed so more of the cell stays intact.

How long does it stay fresh?

Up to 21 days with no preservatives. It also freezes very well.

Where do you ship?

Free shipping on multi-packs to the 48 contiguous states. There's an extra charge for Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico, and we can't ship to PO boxes.
