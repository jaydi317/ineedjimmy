Is it gluten-free?

No. It contains wheat and gluten and is not safe for anyone with celiac disease or a wheat allergy. Many customers with gluten sensitivities tell us they enjoy it, but check with your own doctor first.

What is Whole-Cell Wheat?

Wheat that's gently crushed instead of ground into fine flour, so more of the grain's natural cell structure stays intact. It gives the bread its hearty texture and flavor.

How does it stay fresh 21 days with no preservatives?

That's the Whole-Cell Wheat and the short ingredient list. Keep one loaf out, refrigerate one, and freeze the rest. It freezes very well.

Is your wheat sprayed with glyphosate?

No. We buy from a certified seed farmer who doesn't use glyphosate at any stage of planting, growing or harvest.

Where do you ship?

Free shipping on multi-packs to the 48 contiguous states. There's an extra charge for Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico, and we can't ship to PO boxes.
