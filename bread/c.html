Is it gluten-free?

No. It contains wheat and gluten and is not safe for anyone with celiac disease or a wheat allergy. Many customers with gluten sensitivities tell us they enjoy it, but check with your own doctor first.

How long does it stay fresh?

Up to 21 days with no preservatives. It freezes very well, so keep one out, one in the fridge and one in the freezer.

What's in it?

The original loaf: Whole-Cell Wheat, water, honey, flax, yeast and salt. The sourdough uses date syrup and extra-virgin olive oil.

Where do you ship?

Free shipping on multi-packs to the 48 contiguous states. There's an extra charge for Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico, and we can't ship to PO boxes.
