The chart most people would never show you

A system you can trust is one that tells you what it can't use yet. Every asset carries a permission status, and most of them are not a simple yes.

What we're actually allowed to publish

Status is stored per asset, not decided later by whoever is writing that day.

Status labels are carried on every row of the master matrix. “Pattern fuel” means the language shapes how we write but never appears as a quote.

Anyone can generate more. Almost nobody labels what they can't use.
