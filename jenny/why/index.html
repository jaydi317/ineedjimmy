Why I Do What I Do

Jenny Ervin · in her own words

Jenny — this is a draft, and it’s yours. Every word came out of the three minutes we spent talking. Nothing here is invented. But it’s your name on it, so read it and change anything that isn’t how you’d actually say it. Tell Jimmy what to fix and it gets fixed. Once you’re happy with it, put it at the bottom of your emails.

People don’t come in to buy jewelry for people that they don’t love.

I’ve thought about that for thirty-five years, and it’s the reason I’ve never wanted to do anything else.

Think about who walks through that door. It’s birthdays. It’s engagements. It’s weddings. It’s anniversaries. It’s all happy stuff. Nobody is here on the worst day of their life. Everybody is here because of somebody they love.

Where else can you work where you’re part of somebody’s special occasion?

You can have a rotten day at home. Your family life can be falling apart. You come in here, and it just restores your faith in humanity. That’s not me being sentimental about a job. I’ve watched it happen to me for thirty-five years.

If you asked me what I want to be known for, it’s one word. Kindness. Kindness means a lot to me. That’s the whole thing.

Because here’s what I never forget: people spend their hard-earned money here. Hard-earned. Somebody worked for that. Somebody saved for it. And they’re handing it to me on a day they’re going to remember for the rest of their life.

I’ve never once taken that lightly.

Now, I should tell you the other half, because there is one. I’m a Gemini, so there are two personalities. I am very, very kind — but I am a mama bear. I will protect my kids, and I’m the same way about my customers. I’m a tough mom, but I’m a very loving mom. You’re not going to get away with much. You’re also never going to wonder whether I’m on your side.

And when I can truly help somebody? That makes my day. That’s why I do this. That’s why I’ve done it for thirty-five years.

It’s the best gig ever.

So come see me. Tell me your story first — we’ll get to the ring after. I’d like to be part of your day.

— Jenny
