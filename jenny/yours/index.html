I Need Jimmy

Jenny didn’t pay me
to write that.

I bought a ring from her. Then I asked her three questions for three minutes, and she said something I couldn’t get out of my head:

“People don’t come in to buy jewelry for people that they don’t love.”

Thirty-five years, and nobody had ever written it down.

So I wrote it down.

That’s the whole thing I do. Everybody who’s good at something has one of those sentences in them. Almost nobody can say it about themselves. And the ones who can say it usually don’t have anybody in the room listening.

I listen for a living. I’ve been paid seven figures four separate ways to find the sentence and then sell with it — from a stage, on a webinar, on the phone, on the page.

Jenny’s took three minutes and cost her nothing. I did it because I wanted to.

So — if you’ve been doing your thing a long time and there’s a version of it nobody has ever put into words, tell me below. Two boxes, sixty seconds.

If there’s something there, my AI will text you and get the details. If there’s really something there, you’ll hear from me.

I’m not going to sell you anything over text.

You’ll get a text from Jimmy’s AI. Reply STOP any time and it stops for good.
Your number goes to Jimmy and nowhere else.
