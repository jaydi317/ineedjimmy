Common Questions
Frequently Asked Questions
Do I need a Costco membership to get an eye exam?
No! Our eye care practice is independent — we're located inside Costco, but anyone can book an exam regardless of membership status. You only need a Costco membership if you want to purchase glasses from Costco Optical.
What insurance do you accept?
We currently accept VSP, Spectera/United Healthcare, and Costco employee insurance. We're happy to file claims on your behalf. If you're unsure whether we take your plan, give us a call at (463) 238-2028.
Do you accept walk-ins?
Yes — walk-ins are welcome if our schedule allows. For guaranteed availability, we recommend booking an appointment online or calling ahead. We do ask for 24-hour notice if you need to cancel.
What should I bring to my first appointment?
Please bring your insurance card and ID, current glasses or contacts (if any), a list of medications you're taking, and any previous eye care records if available. If using VSP, we'll need the primary insurer's name, date of birth, and last 4 of their SSN.
What is Optos ultra-widefield imaging?
It's an advanced retinal camera that captures over 185° of the back of your eye in a single image — traditional cameras only get 45°. This means we can detect conditions like diabetic retinopathy, glaucoma, and macular degeneration earlier, often before you notice symptoms.
How often should I get an eye exam?
For most adults, we recommend a comprehensive eye exam every 1-2 years. If you have diabetes, a family history of eye disease, or wear contacts, annual exams are recommended. Children should have their first exam at 6 months, then at age 3, before first grade, and every 1-2 years after.
