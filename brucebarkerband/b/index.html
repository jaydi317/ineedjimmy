Liner notes, in Bruce's words

“I stand on the shoulders of giants.”

“Full band mode. By miles, yours truly will be the least impressive musician on the stage. I surround myself with better company, and people that are uglier, so I look better.”

BZ

Vocals, soprano · lead on 13 songs

My best friend since 1987. She is a sunshine explosion. Miss Positivity. The cup is never half empty, it's always half full.

Susan

Vocals, alto and harmony

When I was 16, she asked me to sing a duet with her in choir. She gave me the confidence that I could be worthy of being on stage. She kick-started everything. She sings harmony like nobody else.

Blake Watts

Drums

Been playing with me since he was 18. He's played with Chris Stapleton, Justin Timberlake, Maren Morris, the Lumineers. Literally the best drummer I've ever heard. As good as he is, his heart and his positivity always come to the forefront.

Scott Pizzera

Bass

A jazz professor. The glue. The maestro. Truly the professor of the band. He picks the keys and checks every chord.

Gary Higgins

Keyboards · films every show

He finds the exact sound an 80s band used and recreates it on one teeny tiny keyboard. What 80s bands needed 15 keyboards for.

Michael Kelsey

Guitar

Voted the best unsigned guitarist in America. Steve Vai tried to sign him on the spot. He's just so tasty with all his licks.

JC Hatton

Vocals, high tenor

We've sung together since Purdue, around 1989. He's never had a bad day in his life. Pour Some Sugar on Me is JC's song now.

David Beering

Trumpet · sits in 5–6 times a night

Photo coming soon. Listen for the horn.
