Strategy Brief · Prepared for Karen Seleen

Transformations by Karen

From "the salon" to a business that fits your life — and finally feels like you.
The business, in one sentence

You help women who were made to feel invisible — told they were "the ugly one," or simply never shown how — finally feel seen, beautiful, and comfortable in their own skin, from the inside out.

The one woman you serve

You said it without hesitation: the woman who was told she was ugly is the one who makes your heart race. So we build the whole business around her. Not "women who want a closet cleanout" — her.

Where she is now

She's stopped trying. She skips the mirror. She wears the same safe three outfits. Somewhere — from a parent, a comment, or just years of being overlooked — she decided she's "not the pretty one," so why bother. She feels invisible, and she's quietly convinced no one would notice if she changed.

Where you take her

In your words: joyful, confident, happier — comfortable in her own skin. She looks in the mirror and, maybe for the first time, likes the woman looking back. She feels seen. That's the product. The clothes and makeup are just how you get her there.

Why no one can copy you

There are stylists everywhere. There is exactly one of you. For two decades, women in your chair have wanted three things from you that had nothing to do with hair — and you named all three yourself: safety, an ear that listens, and someone to finally teach them what no one ever did. That's not a service. That's a gift. It's also proof you've been doing this work for years without charging for the part that matters most.

Your signature method

1

Seen

You listen. You create the safety no one gave her. She's finally heard — no judgment, no "you should have."

2

Shown

You teach what no one taught her: her colors, her cuts, a handful of outfits, her own makeup. The practical magic.

3

Shining

She turns to the mirror and there she is — radiant, confident, seen. The reveal that makes women cry in your studio.

The offer ladder

  • The Becoming Session
    A private half-day in your studio — the listening, the colors & cuts, the outfits, the makeup, the reveal. This is your front door and your testimonial machine.
    ~$297
  • The 90-Day Becoming
    The full journey for the woman ready to step all the way into her next chapter. In-person core, with Zoom/phone for the weeks between or for women who live far away.
    ~$1,997
  • The Becoming Circle · (later)
    A small group or simple course once you have testimonials — so your wisdom can reach more women without draining you. This is where the "no standing on my feet" dream lives.
    future

The model that fits your actual life

You told me the truth: three days a week, roughly 10 to 4, no early mornings, no nights, no weekends. Good. That's not a limitation — it's the design. This is a premium, low-volume business. A few deeply-served women at a real price beats a packed calendar at hairdresser prices. In-person is your heart; Zoom is simply the option for the woman who lives too far or whose hours don't line up.

Your first three moves

  1. Collect your proof. Go back to the three women you've already transformed — the one who was overweight, the one whose mom said "ugly," the one no one ever taught — and ask for a few sentences and a photo. You already did the work. Now capture it.
  2. Run three Becoming Sessions. Start with women you know. The first one or two at a soft price in exchange for an honest testimonial. Get your reps, refine your flow, take before/afters.
  3. Pick your name & go live. Choose the direction that feels like you (see the two website mockups), and let me build the real page. You don't need everything perfect — you need to begin.
A gentle note, because it matters: you're an empath, and that's your superpower — but it's also why you come home empty. Build this for that, not against it. Few women, real prices, firm hours, and a group/course down the road so you're not carrying everyone one-on-one forever. Protect the woman in your mirror, too.
